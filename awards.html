Dr. C P Ali Bava Haji

Chairman and Managing Trustee

An esteemed authority with a rich and wide experience in the various Business, Educational and Philanthropic activities. He has developed his ventures in India, Middle East and Mozambique. As the chairman of Crescent International Group, he has presence in different verticals like infrastructure Development, Tourism, Manufacturing, Retail, Educational Activities.

He is the Chairman of Eranad Knowledge City, Malabar Academic City, Malabar Dental college and Research Centre, Al Ameen engineering College, Al Ameen law college, Modern Higher Secondary School and Crescent International Group, Dubai.
