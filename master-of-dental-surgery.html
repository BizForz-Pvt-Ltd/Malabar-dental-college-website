Master of Dental Surgery (MDS)

MDS course is a master level degree for student who have graduated in Dental Surgery.

Master of Dental Surgery courses started in Malabar Dental College and Research Centre in 2016. The institution offers MDS courses in 7 specialities :

  • Oral Medicine and Radiology
  • Prosthodontics and Crown & Bridge
  • Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics
  • Pedodontics & Preventive Dentistry
  • Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics
  • Oral Pathology and Microbiology
  • Periodontology

The first MDS batch passed out successfully in 2019.
