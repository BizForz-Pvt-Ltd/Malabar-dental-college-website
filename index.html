About Us

MDCRC is specifically designed for all dental care aspirants who want to land in the profession of dental care with smart and quality education. North Kerala's first NAAC accredited Dental Institution.

This educational institution was started in 2007 under the University of Calicut. In 2010, it was truly blessed to come under the aegis of Kerala University of Health Sciences, Thrissur. The beautiful campus situated near to the banks of Bharathapuzha adds to the beauty of the campus, which spreads over 25 acres of land. Its lush green and pollution-free campus is well designed and graciously equipped with all modern facilities. MDCRC offers academic programs from undergraduate to postgraduate program in seven specialties. The institution also offers Diploma courses in three streams and PhD in five specialities. Collectively, our students and residents also benefit from the skills and experience of our highly devoted faculty, and a rich collaborative environment.

Recognized College

MDCRC is recognized by Dental Council of India & Affiliated to Kerala University of Health Sciences since 2006.
