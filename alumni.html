Alumni Objectives

  • To promote and create awareness about career advancement and placement options available to students after the course.
  • To conduct activities that will help the students to get exposure to the current trends of the dentistry by way of knowledge, training, internship and placement.
  • To Continuously upgrade the knowledge of students to make them abreast with recent trends of dentistry through lectures, seminars, workshops etc.
  • To guide the students for preparations of various competitive exams like PG-NEET for MDS course admission etc.
  • To inform students about the available job oppurtunities in the government sectors and off campus drives and to arrange motivational talks.

Career Advancement (Training Guidance activities)

Career guidance cell in MDCRC offers valuable support and information to students regarding their professional journey. It provides insights into various specializations, job opportunities, postgraduate options, and industry trends within the field of dentistry. The cell helps students make informed decisions about their career paths, assisting them in choosing the right direction based on their interests, strengths, and goals. We organize workshops, seminars, and guest lectures to expose students to diverse aspects of dentistry and connect them with experienced professionals. Overall, the career guidance cell plays a crucial role in shaping the future of dental students and facilitating their successful transition into the dental industry.
